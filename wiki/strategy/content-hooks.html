Hook bank from the August 2026 report. "Loved" and "Liked" appear to be Daniele's own ratings inside the BraveBrand app, which makes this the best available signal of Daniele's voice preferences until real writing samples arrive.

Patterns in the "loved" set

  • Rent vs own framing dominates (see digital real estate).
  • Website as employee or sales team, never as brochure.
  • Direct second-person address to the owner ("You built a great business, now…").
  • Contrast structure: "Stop X, start Y".
  • Audience call-outs: "For the overthinkers who…", "To the business owner who…".
  • Concrete curiosity: "I audited 50 local business websites…", "this one overlooked setting".
  • Time-boxed transformation: 30 days.

Caution

Several hooks contain claims that are unsourced: "I audited 50 local business websites", "$50k in revenue by fixing their Google profile", "5 new inquiries", "30 days", "60 days". Do not publish these until a real proof point backs them. See proof/ once populated.
