Daniele Manca is a web designer, developer, and digital strategy consultant based in Leyton, East London. Trades as "Daniele Manca — Digital Strategy" (site footer) and appears on Google as "Daniele Manca", category Web Designer.

Background (Daniele, 2026-09-03)

  • 18 years in the industry overall; 12+ years in web development.
  • Background in online ad management as well as web design and development, which is why ads sit alongside design, development, and SEO in the offer.
  • Runs a solo agency. Site copy alternates between "I" and "we"; "I" is accurate.

Facts

  • Location: 92B Grange Park Rd, London E10 5ES (Leyton). Works across Hackney, Haringey, Islington, and throughout London; "and further afield" per the About page.
  • Website: danielemanca.co.uk (WordPress). Phone: 07810 861035.
  • Google rating: 5.0 from 10 reviews.
  • Skills clients name: WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce to Shopify migration, front-end development, design sensibility plus technical skill, SEO, ads.
  • Positioning statement on site: "Calm Authority, Broader Reach".
  • Serves small and medium businesses in London, any niche (Daniele, 2026-09-03). The outgoing site narrows this to professional service firms and established e-commerce; that is superseded.

What clients say about the person

Professional, patient, always available, clear and practical, attention to detail, local and willing to meet in person, "a rare combination of design sensibility and technical development skill".
