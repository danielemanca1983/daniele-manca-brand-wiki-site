The three foundational beliefs the brand stands on, chosen by Daniele on 2026-09-16 from candidates distilled out of the four content pillar points. They sit at the brand level alongside the One Word (Bleeding to Working, see positioning) and above the pillars. Three, no more. Everything the brand says reinforces one of them.

1. Everything you've been told about marketing your local business is rent.

Ads, boosted posts, marketplaces, "lead gen". None of it is yours. The day you stop paying, it stops. Reinforced by pillar 1 (Own, don't rent) and the digital real estate metaphor. Hooks that carry it: "Stop paying Google rent for traffic you should own", "What happens when you stop renting your customers from Google Ads?"

2. You don't have a traffic problem. You've never had one.

More visitors to a site that leaks just means bleeding faster. Reinforced by pillar 2 (A site that works) and The 11pm Test. Hooks that carry it: "Your website doesn't need more traffic, it needs a better foundation", "A pretty website is useless if it doesn't generate leads."

3. Cheap doesn't exist. There's only paying once or paying twice.

The £500 template, DIY or AI site is not a saving, it is a deposit on the rebuild. Reinforced by pillar 3 (The real cost of cheap), The 18-Month Cliff and the cost guide. Hook that carries it: "The hidden cost of a 'cheap' website design." As with pillar 3, "cheap" means template, DIY and AI-generated, not the £750 Simple tier.

How they relate to pillar 4

Found where they're looking has no core message of its own by design. It serves messages 1 and 2: being found is the owned alternative to renting, and being found only pays if the site does not leak.
