Current positioning: The Digital Landlord. Confirmed by Daniele on 2026-09-03. The live website still carries the older "Website-as-an-Asset" positioning and is scheduled to be replaced.

Statement (working draft, from the offer statement)

For local business owners who are tired of being invisible, Daniele Manca turns your website into a 24/7 sales team so you can stop renting traffic from Google and start owning your local market.

Pillars

  • Own, don't rent. Ads and boosted posts are rent. A website plus local search presence is property that keeps producing. See digital real estate.
  • The website is your best employee. Not a brochure, not a "digital paperweight". It should generate inquiries while you sleep.
  • Local and in person. Leyton, East London. Clients value meeting face to face and finding him on Google.
  • Design and development in one person. 18 years in the industry, 12+ in web development, plus online ad management. A solo agency, so the person you talk to does the work.
  • Jargon-free, education first. Explains the why. Fills the "education-first" gap identified in market gaps.
  • AI-ready presence. Visible in Google and in AI search summaries, a differentiator against legacy SEO agencies. The Advanced and Pro tiers also put an AI concierge on the client's own site to qualify leads, which makes the "24/7 sales team" promise literal.

What carries over from Website-as-an-Asset

The asset idea itself, the anti-hype and anti-cheapest-option stance, the free strategy call as the entry point, and the honest pricing-band education. Only the register and the audience change: louder, more direct, and open to any SMB. Historical detail in Website-as-an-Asset.
