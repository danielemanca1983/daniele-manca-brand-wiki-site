As told on the About page. This is the public version, so it is framed as a philosophy shift rather than a personal timeline.

Timeline facts (Daniele, 2026-09-03)

18 years in the industry, starting in online ad management, then 12+ years in web design and development. Now a solo agency in Leyton. The ads-first background explains the recurring theme of wasted ad spend on weak websites.

The problem Daniele kept seeing

Websites that look "done" on the surface but underneath are unclear, over-complicated, or misaligned with the business. Decisions made reactively. Vendors speaking in abstractions. Responsibility fragmented, nobody accountable. The owner is left with quiet hesitation: is this working, are we leaving money on the table, what breaks when traffic spikes?

Why standard approaches fail

Two bad options for the owner: "just trust the experts" with no clarity, or be handed tools and dashboards and expected to become technical overnight. Daniele's line: "Websites don't fail because owners don't care. They fail because strategy, stewardship, and accountability are missing."

The turning point

Stop treating the website as a design exercise or a collection of tools. Treat it as a business asset that protects credibility, supports revenue, and reduces risk. "Instead of chasing trends, you build structure. Instead of reacting, you steward." That idea became The Website-as-an-Asset System.
