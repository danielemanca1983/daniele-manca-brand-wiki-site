What the ICP has already tried and been burned by. Each is a latent objection: "why will you be different?"

Failed solutions

  • DIY website builders (Wix, Squarespace) that look "amateurish"
  • Cheap offshore SEO that delivered zero results
  • Boosting Facebook posts with no conversion strategy
  • Google Ads spend with no calls ("$2k on Google Ads and got zero calls. Never again.")

Confirmed by real clients (Google reviews)

  • Previous web designers who failed to deliver: "years of hopping from one web designer to another with failed attempts at websites".
  • Doubt that a fair price can buy good work: "I wasn't sure if he can do a good job with the price range we agreed".

Implied objections

  • Scepticism toward anyone selling SEO or lead generation ("snake oil", "scams")
  • Fear of contracts that are hard to exit (see Thryv complaints in competitive landscape)
  • Fear of paying for something they cannot measure
