How the ICP describes their problem and their goal. Use these phrases verbatim in copy.

Real client language (Google reviews, verbatim)

  • "struggling for years to find the right web developer who matches my vision"
  • "years of hopping from one web designer to another with failed attempts"
  • "I wasn't sure if he can do a good job with the price range we agreed"
  • "exceeded my expectations" (twice)
  • "clear, practical, and insightful advice", "clear feasible solutions"
  • "clean, modern, and functional"
  • "patient, and always available to answer my questions"
  • "a local Word Press developer that I found on google"
  • "meet in person to discuss the website's design in detail"
