Ranked pain points of the primary ICP, from the August 2026 market intelligence report. Quotes marked synthesized were generated by the research tool and are not real customer words. Quotes attributed to Reddit are real-world sourced but unverified.

Pain Severity Emotional state
Outdated website destroys trust critical Embarrassment referring people to their own site
Wasted ad spend critical Feeling scammed by digital marketing
Invisible in local search moderate Anxiety about losing local share
Difficulty scaling e-commerce moderate Stuck at a revenue plateau
Technical overwhelm low Paralysed by jargon and tech stacks

Confirmed by real clients

  • Burned by previous designers (Kai Sahin) — supports the "wasted spend / scepticism" pain.
  • Wanting a local person they can meet (Eren) — a pain the report misses: distrust of remote or offshore providers.
