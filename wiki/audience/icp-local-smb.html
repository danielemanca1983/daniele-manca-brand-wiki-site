Daniele's ideal clients are local small to medium-sized businesses that want digital expertise so their online presence becomes a "24/7 sales team", brings in more inquiries, and makes more money. This is Daniele's own statement of the target audience (the one line in the source written by Daniele rather than generated).

Who they are

  • Location: London. Daniele serves small to medium sized businesses in London, with no industry niche (Daniele, 2026-09-03).
  • Owner-operated local businesses with a physical location or a local service area.
  • Both service businesses and small e-commerce operators (the source mentions WooCommerce and Shopify frustrations).
  • Not technical. They want to run their business, not manage plugins or learn code.

Who actually buys (evidence from reviews and portfolio)

  • Local trades: Black Horse Electrical, ZJ Electrical Services.
  • Small e-commerce owners on Shopify or WooCommerce (Luca F, Stellar Hair, Canturi).
  • Small business owners wanting a first proper site (Jay Dodah, Kai Sahin).
  • Professional service and B2B firms (Biz Optimisers, Eaton Syalon recruitment).
  • Occasional large or enterprise work (Ecobank front-end), probably not the ICP.

Segment decision (resolved 2026-09-03)

ICP is small and medium sized businesses in any niche, in London. The outgoing website's narrower targeting (professional service firms and established e-commerce, excluding early-stage businesses) is superseded. Reviews already show the real mix: trades, small e-commerce, first-site owners, and service firms.

Current state

Overwhelmed by "digital noise". Juggling manual operations. Website looks dated or does not convert. Invisible in local search. Feel they are "throwing money" at ads with no clear return, which has left them sceptical and burnt out.

Desired state

A "set it and forget it" digital engine. Waking up to a steady stream of qualified leads. A professional presence that builds instant trust. A clear line from marketing spend to revenue.

Core problem

"Invisible Business" syndrome: a real business that is absent or irrelevant when local customers search on Google or AI tools. Urgency comes from losing share daily to competitors who appear in the Local Pack or AI search summaries.

Emotional triggers

invisible, trust, automated, professional, growth
