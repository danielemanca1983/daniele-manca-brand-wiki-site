Hero

Stop paying Google rent for customers you should own.

The Digital Landlord turns your website into a 24/7 sales team for your local business. Built and looked after by one person in East London who has done this for 18 years.

[Book a free strategy call]

The problem

You built a great business. But when someone in your area searches for what you do, they find the guy down the street.

So you do what everyone does. You boost a post. You put £500 into Google Ads. The phone rings for a week, then it stops the moment the money does. That is not marketing. That is rent. And the landlord is Google.

Meanwhile your website sits there looking like it was built in 2010, and you quietly avoid sending people to it.

The reframe

A website is not a brochure and it is not an expense. It is property.

Done right, it is the one employee that never calls in sick: it shows up on Google Maps, answers the questions your customers have at 11pm, and hands you the enquiry in the morning. Every month you own it, it gets more valuable. Every month you rent traffic instead, you start again from zero.

SEO is not a tech headache. It is digital property management. You do not need to understand it. You need someone to manage the property.

That is the job.

What you get

1. The storefront. A website designed to build trust in seconds and turn visitors into enquiries. Clear message, obvious next step, works on a phone, loads fast. Built on WordPress or Shopify, depending on the business.

2. The location. Local search foundations so you show up when your neighbourhood searches: Google Business Profile set up and optimised, the technical groundwork Google and AI search tools need, pages built around what your customers actually type.

3. The property manager (optional, from £50 a month). Weekly check-ups and plugin updates, so the property never falls into disrepair. Not included in the build price; add it if you want the site looked after.

4. Ads, only when they make sense (optional add-on). If you want faster results while the property matures, paid traffic set up so it lands on a site that converts, with clear expectations and no wasted spend.

How it works

  1. Free 30-minute strategy call. We look at your current site and your Google presence, and I tell you what is holding you back. No pitch. If it is not a fit, you still leave knowing what to do.
  2. Build. From four weeks, depending on the tier. You get one point of contact from start to finish, because I do the design and the development myself.
  3. Own it. The site, the domain, and the Google profile are yours. No lock-in contracts, no platform you cannot leave.

Who this is for

  • Local business owners in London who are tired of being the best-kept secret in town
  • Trades, shops, clinics, studios, services: any niche, as long as your customers are local
  • Owners who want more enquiries without becoming a part-time marketer
  • Anyone embarrassed to send a customer to their current website

Who this is not for

  • Anyone looking for the cheapest option
  • Anyone who wants a guaranteed ranking by Friday
  • Businesses that want to keep renting

Questions you might have

I have spent money on this before and got nothing. So have most of my clients before they came to me. The difference is what you are buying. Ads and cheap SEO are rent. This is property, and you keep it.

I am not technical. Good. You should not have to be. You will get plain English and one person to call.

Can I leave? Yes. The site, the domain, and the Google profile are yours.

Do you only do WordPress? WordPress and Shopify, including moving from one to the other.

Where are you based? Leyton, East London. Happy to meet in person across London.
