The single call to action across the whole site. 30 minutes, video call, free, no preparation required. "Come with questions. Leave with clarity."

What it covers

  • How the website currently supports or undermines credibility
  • Where performance, conversion, or trust may be leaking
  • What needs attention and what doesn't
  • Whether the flagship system or a single service is the right fit

Structure

  1. Discuss the business, goals, and what the website is expected to do.
  2. Identify gaps, risks, or misalignment across design, development, SEO, ads, strategy.
  3. Talk through realistic next steps: one service, a combination, or the full system.

"You'll leave with a clearer understanding of your situation — even if we don't work together."

What it is not

A pressure-filled sales call, a generic audit or checklist, jargon or upselling. "That clarity protects everyone's time."

Fallbacks

Contact form (interest options: Web Design, Web Development, Website-as-an-Asset) and WhatsApp.
