From the 2026 cost guide article. Useful because it is Daniele's own market education and anchors where his offers sit.

Band What it is Daniele's framing
£300 to £800 Template or AI-builder site Cheap up front, then £200 to £500 a year in fixes, often rebuilt within 18 months
£1,000 to £2,500 Custom freelance site
£3,000 to £6,000 Strategy-led design and development The recommended tier
£8,000 to £20,000+ E-commerce and bespoke builds
£50 to £200 a month Hosting, security, backups, minor changes Ongoing cost to factor in

Stated sweet spot for most East London small businesses: £3,000 to £5,000 for a strategy-led site. "Below that, you're usually buying aesthetics without substance."

The outgoing floors line up with the bands: design from £1,000, development from £1,500, flagship from £3,500.

Contradiction with the new offer (logged 2026-09-03)

The Digital Landlord's Simple tier starts at £750, inside the "template or AI website" band this article tells readers to avoid, and well below the article's stated £3,000 floor for substance. Daniele's decision is newer and wins for pricing. Daniele declined to revise the cost guide for now (2026-09-03). Lint should re-raise this when the new site goes live.
