Status: the current offer, under the Daniele Manca brand. Deliverables, structure, timeline, and pricing floor confirmed by Daniele on 2026-09-03. Approved page copy: offer page.

Deliverables

  1. The storefront. Website designed to build trust fast and convert visitors into enquiries. WordPress or Shopify.
  2. The location. Local search foundations: Google Business Profile, technical groundwork for Google and AI search, pages built around what local customers search.
  3. The property manager. Optional monthly plan from £50 a month: weekly check-ups and plugin updates. Not included in any tier.
  4. Ads. Optional add-on. Set-up from £500 plus media spend.

Structure and pricing

Tier Includes From
Simple Trust-building site with local search foundations £750
Advanced 10+ pages; AI concierge that qualifies leads; blog with 3 tailor-written posts £1,500
Pro Everything in Advanced; tailored FAQ page; location pages and project pages by niche; custom functionality such as a quotation tool TBD

Timeline from 4 weeks. Every project starts with the free strategy consultation.

Promises

  • No lock-in: the site, domain, and Google profile belong to the client, who is free to leave.
  • One person does the design and the development.

Offer statement (app-generated, still the working statement): For local business owners who are tired of being invisible, The Digital Landlord provides a high-converting property asset system that turns your website into a 24/7 sales team so you can stop renting traffic from Google and start owning your local market.

Benefits

  • A professional digital storefront that builds instant trust with every visitor
  • A local search presence that puts the business in front of customers ready to buy
  • A predictable lead-generation engine that removes the need for constant ad spend
  • A clear, jargon-free roadmap to owning your digital real estate

Use cases

  • Stop losing local share to competitors who show up first on Google Maps
  • Convert casual visitors into qualified inquiries while you sleep
  • Replace expensive, ineffective ad campaigns with a permanent organic asset
  • Remove the embarrassment of an outdated, amateur-looking website

Hidden benefits

  • Reclaimed mental energy previously lost to tech stress
  • Pride in a brand identity that matches the quality of the service
  • A sense of stability and control over the business's future

Relationship to the outgoing offer

The live website still sells The Website-as-an-Asset System from £3,500. That site is being replaced. Pricing floors from the outgoing offer and the pricing bands article are the best available pricing reference until Daniele sets Digital Landlord pricing.
