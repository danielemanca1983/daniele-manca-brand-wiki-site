The organising metaphor of The Digital Landlord, confirmed as the go-forward positioning on 2026-09-03. Daniele's own published articles already use the expense-vs-asset and brochure-vs-lead-generator framing, so the metaphor has roots in his writing, not just the app's output.

The idea

  • Paying for ads is renting traffic and customers from Google and Facebook. The moment you stop paying, the flow stops.
  • A website plus local search presence is property. Investment compounds, and it keeps producing without rent.
  • Daniele's role is the landlord or property manager: the person who builds and maintains the asset so the owner does not have to.
  • SEO reframed as "digital property management" rather than a "tech headache".
  • The website reframed as an employee: "your most valuable employee", "a 24/7 sales team", "works as hard as you do", not a brochure or a "digital paperweight".

Sub-metaphors in use

  • storefront, digital home, digital real estate, property asset
  • renting vs owning, paying Google rent
  • expense vs asset
  • invisible vs local authority

Relationship to Website-as-an-Asset

The live website uses the same asset idea in a calmer voice (infrastructure, stewardship). See Website-as-an-Asset and the contradiction logged in positioning.

Why it works for this ICP

Local business owners already understand rent, property, and staff. The metaphor turns an abstract technical service into a familiar business decision.
