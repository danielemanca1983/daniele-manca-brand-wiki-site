Thryv

  • Positioning: all-in-one business management software for small businesses
  • Pricing: subscription
  • Strengths: massive brand awareness, all-in-one feature set
  • Weaknesses: expensive, poor customer support
  • Complaint: "The contract is impossible to get out of." — Trustpilot

Generic local SEO agencies

  • Positioning: rank-focused local marketing
  • Pricing: varies
  • Strengths: specialised focus
  • Weaknesses: lack of transparency, cookie-cutter strategies
  • Complaint: "They promise the world and deliver nothing but spammy backlinks." — Reddit r/localmarketing

Indirect competitors (from failed solutions)

  • Wix, Squarespace and other DIY builders
  • Cheap offshore SEO providers
  • Facebook boosted posts and Google Ads as a substitute for an owned asset
