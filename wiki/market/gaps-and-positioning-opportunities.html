Gaps

  • Personalised, high-touch service for local businesses that feel ignored by big agencies.
  • Education-first marketing that explains the "why" behind digital changes.

Positioning opportunities

  • Be the "Digital Partner", not a "Service Provider".
  • Own "AI-ready" local presence (visibility in AI search summaries, not just Google) to differentiate from legacy SEO agencies.

Both opportunities align with the digital real estate framing: a partner who helps you own an asset, rather than a vendor who rents you traffic.
